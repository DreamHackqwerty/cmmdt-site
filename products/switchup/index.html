Replace Mac app icons with your own images using SwitchUp. Download the Apple Silicon or Intel version and fit custom icons to the macOS icon grid.

Icon customizer

SwitchUp

SwitchUp lets you change the icon of any app on your Mac. Drop in a PNG, pick the app, and apply. Your image is fitted to the macOS icon grid automatically.

  • Drag and drop a PNG and an .app
  • Optional rounded mask and shadow
  • Fits the macOS icon grid
  • Restore the original icon any time
