A Fisch macro built for Mac

ALware Quasar is CMMDT’s free Fisch macro for Mac. It reads colors on your screen to automate fishing in Roblox Fisch, with separate downloads for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Quasar v3.5.0 adds rod support, automatic utilities and a redesigned status HUD.

Can I use a Fisch macro on a MacBook?

Yes. ALware has macOS builds for MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, as well as desktop Macs. Open Apple menu → About This Mac to check your hardware: choose Apple Silicon if it lists an Apple chip, or Intel Mac if it lists an Intel processor. The Windows download is a separate v3.0 release.

How do I get started with ALware on Mac?

  1. Download the v3.5.0 .dmg above that matches your Mac’s chip, then open it to install ALware.
  2. Launch ALware and follow its setup prompts for macOS permissions. Watch the Mac setup tutorial for a walkthrough.
  3. Open Fisch in Roblox, select your rod preset and configure the detection regions for your screen before starting the macro.

What makes the best Fisch macro for Mac?

The best Fisch macro for your Mac depends on your chip, rod and setup. Check for a macOS build, support for the rods you use, adjustable detection settings and clear setup guidance. ALware offers color-based tracking, rod presets, named region sets and adjustable automation. Watch the ALware showcase to see whether its workflow fits how you play.

What does Quasar v3.5.0 include?

Supported rod additions include Lullaby, Halibut Harpoon, Tranquility Rod, Bellona’s Waraxe, Nate’s Blade and DreamBreaker. Auto Utility V2 adds backpack search, automatic weather and time changes, and hunt alerts. Active and passive harpooning, Discord catch webhooks and a status HUD with catch counts are also included. Is ALware free, and where can I get help?

ALware is free to download. CMMDT develops and maintains the app; the GitHub releases contain the downloads and version history. For setup questions or bug reports, contact the team through CMMDT Discord or visit the team page.
