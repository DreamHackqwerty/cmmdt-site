Hotbar

Drag a unit onto a slot to equip it · drag between slots to swap · drag a slot off the hotbar to unequip. Keys 1–6 pick these in battle.

Equip rules and rewards
  • Flying enemies: air units (Gunner, Cupid, Galactic Overlord) sit on the raised hills by the road and hit both flying and ground enemies. Hybrids hit both too: Euphrosyne (anywhere), Professor Fission and Tesla Coil, the Necromancer’s Fists, Swords and Odin, and the Frozen Emperor as the Frost Dragon. Poseidon, Mortar, TideCrasher and Cryo Spire hit ground only.
  • Status effects: Bleed, Burning, Frostbite and Lovestruck deal damage over time · Crippled and Wet slow enemies · Frozen, Lovestruck and Electrified stun · Crippled enemies take +15% damage · making an Electrified enemy Wet deals a big shock. Select a unit to see what it inflicts.
  • Hotbar limits: 1 godly, 1 mythic (2 with Euphrosyne in slot 6) and up to 3 legendary units. Select an evolvable unit (Mortar, Oil Rig, Cryo Spire, TideCrasher) to see its Evolve tab.
  • Every cleared wave gives 20 rerolls (500 in Infinite). Every 20th Infinite wave gives 5 Ultra Rolls.
  • Evolution materials: Condensed Water (5 per Infinite wave), Leviathan Shell (25% from the Leviathan raid), Oil Barrel (50% from clearing Ash Wastes), Purified Toxic Core (100% from the Colossus raid), Frozen Scale (clearing Frost Pass), Ice Shrapnel (10 per wave in Frost Pass Infinite), Subzero Catalyst (Frost Dragon's Lair raid), Purified Uranium (50% from clearing Dust Road), Purified Plutonium (35% from clearing Ash Wastes), Scrap Metal (2 per wave on Dust Road or Ash Wastes).
  • Locked units show where they drop on their card.
